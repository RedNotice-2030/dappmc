1. HOSPITAL OVERVIEW & HISTORY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Hospital Name:** Dr. Arturo P. Pingoy Medical Center (DAPPMC)
**Hospital Type:** 100-bed tertiary hospital
**Established:** May 12, 1961, by spouses Arturo P. Pingoy, MD and Amparo Y. Pingoy, MD
**Current Address:** Gensan Drive, Koronadal, 9506 South Cotabato, Philippines

**History Timeline:**
- 1961: Hospital established by spouses Arturo P. Pingoy, MD and Amparo Y. Pingoy, MD.
- 1971: Four private air-conditioned rooms were put up.
- 1974: A two-storey building was constructed housing 8 private rooms and 4 suite rooms.
- 1998: Bed capacity expanded to 50 with the construction of 40 private rooms financed by the Land Bank of the Philippines.
- 2005: Upgraded to a tertiary hospital with the construction of the Administrative building; became a 100-bed hospital.
- May 2012: Construction of a new building began; expansion toward becoming the premier and largest hospital with a 3-storey building (to house patient rooms, doctors' clinics, commercial spaces, and a medical arts area).
- Accreditation: PhilHealth — Center of Safety and Center of Quality.
- Certification: ISO 9001:2015 for quality management systems.

**Vision:** Delivering Exceptional Care with Compassion.
**Mission:** Advancing Life and the Environment through Innovation and Responsible Healthcare.
**Core Values:** Integrity, Compassion, Accountability, Reliability, and Excellence.
**IMS Policy:** Committed to quality healthcare, compliance with regulatory requirements, continual improvement of the Integrated Management System (IMS), and protecting the environment through responsible resource use and pollution prevention.

11. ADMISSION PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Admission Steps:**
1. Enter the Emergency Room. (Only two watchers are allowed in the emergency room to avoid overcrowding.)
2. Initial Assessment: The Emergency Room Nurse performs an initial assessment of the patient and instructs the watcher to proceed to the admitting section.
3. Proceed to Admitting Section: The watcher proceeds to the admitting section for encoding of the patient's personal information and presents the pertinent documents.
4. Gather Admission Data: The Admitting Section gathers admission data.
5. Document and Register: The Admitting Section documents and registers the patient.
6. Forward Admission Data: The Admitting Section forwards and presents the admission data to the Emergency Room.

**Documents to bring for admission:**
- Valid ID
- HMO card / insurance details (if applicable)
- Doctor's referral or request (if applicable)
- Any previous medical records or test results

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
12. YAKAP PROCEDURE
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
YAKAP is a PhilHealth benefit program for newborns/children at the YAKAP Center.
**Steps:**
1. Arrival at the YAKAP Center to begin the process.
2. Registration and presentation of required documents.
3. Initial screening by YAKAP personnel.
4. Medical consultation with a YAKAP physician.
5. Referral to diagnostic centers (if needed).
6. Return to the YAKAP physician with diagnostic results or follow-up status.
7. Prescription and treatment plan from the physician.
8. Processing and signing of administrative documents.
9. Discharge and final instructions.

14. PATIENT RIGHTS & RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
**Patient Rights:**
- Access to Care, Transfer, and Continuity of Care: access to available medical treatment and services; if necessary care cannot be provided, referral to another facility with the reason explained.
- Respect and Dignity: compassionate and respectful care recognizing dignity, with protection from abuse, neglect, and exploitation.
- Privacy and Confidentiality: privacy and confidentiality protected during treatment; medical records cannot be released without consent.
- Medical Record: medical records are confidential and not released without written consent or a court order.
- Information: the right to know the attending physician and receive complete, up-to-date information on diagnosis, treatment, and prognosis.
- Communication: the right to visitors and to communicate with people outside the hospital, verbally or in writing.
- Acceptance and Refusal of Treatment: the right to accept or refuse medical or surgical treatment, including resuscitative services.
- Access to Protective Services: entitled to a safe, clean, and secure hospital environment.

**Patient Responsibilities:**
- Following Rules and Regulations: including the No Smoking Policy and the policy for children under nine (9) years old.
- Providing Information: provide complete and accurate information that may affect care; report perceived risks or unexpected changes in condition.
- Complying with Treatment Plans and Instructions: follow the treatment plan and ask questions if instructions are unclear; bear responsibility for consequences of not following the prescribed plan.
- Showing Respect and Consideration: respect hospital staff, property, and other patients; do not bring alcoholic beverages, drugs, firearms, or deadly weapons into the premises.
- Meeting Financial Commitments: meet financial obligations promptly as agreed with the hospital.
